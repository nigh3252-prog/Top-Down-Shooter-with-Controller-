ARCANA · PHONE DEMO

      
Left half — drag anywhere to move.

      
Right buttons — tap to cast forward, or drag off the button to aim and release.

      
Blazing Lariat slides you forward and spins two long fire whips. Fill the SIGNATURE meter by
         dealing damage, then cast it charged for three persistent whips you can walk away from.

      
Explosive Charge stores three dashes. Every dummy you cross takes contact damage and a
         one-second explosive that detonates along the direction of that dash.

      
      
Desktop: WASD / arrows to move · J = Lariat · K = Charge · mouse aims
