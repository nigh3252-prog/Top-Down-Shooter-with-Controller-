## 13. Ice Dagger

- **Gate 1 element:** Ice
- **Gate 1 category:** Basic
- **Evidence:** documented-online
- **Evidence label:** [DOCUMENTED]
- **Source:** [Wizard of Legend Wiki — Ice Dagger](https://wizardoflegend.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Dagger)
- **Wiki element:** Water (Ice)
- **Wiki type:** Basic
- **Wiki subtypes:** Melee, Movement
- **Wiki stats:** Element: Water (Ice); Type: Basic; Subtypes: Melee, Movement; Damage: 6, 6, 8, 10; Hit Count: 4 per combo; Knockback: 14, 14, 16, 20; Cooldown: 0; Cost: 17 50; Pool: 4
- **Wiki enhanced stats:** Damage: 6, 6, 8, 10, 12 (ice shard); Hit Count: 5 per combo
- **Wiki behavior:** [DOCUMENTED] Move forward while rapidly stabbing enemies with conjured ice daggers. Each combo of this basic arcana consists of four casts, with each dagger dealing increasing damage: 6, 6, 8, and 10 f enhanced, the attack conjures two ice daggers side by side. On the final hit, two shards of ice are shot forward as piercing projectiles, dealing 12 damage and passing through all enemies before dissipating just before reaching the edge of the screen. The attack button can be held to perform a full combo of stabs.
- **Wiki enhanced behavior:** [DOCUMENTED] Throws out a piercing dagger on the final strike!
- **Wiki strategy notes:** [DOCUMENTED] Due to its narrow area, Ice Dagger struggles against spread out enemies. To mitigate this, try to tightly group up enemies to ensure that all are hit or single out an enemy by immobilizing the rest (via, freeze, entanglement, shock etc) and focusing the single one down. Moving your aim when performing a combo allows you to steer the direction of your strikes. This is useful when maneuvering enemies with its knockback or switching targets to another enemy if one dies mid combo. The movement while performing a combo can make it difficult to maintain damage on a single target (such as a Boss or Miniboss). Movement while casting can be prevented by positioning the mouse cursor very close to your character or, if you are playing on a controller, by returning the stick to the neutral position after aiming towards the enemy. A simple yet effective strategy with Ice Daggers is to hit a group of enemies with the basic combo, then dash through them to get behind and preform another basic combo, moving them back towards where you started. This can be repeated as long as you want and can keep enemies stun locked due to the speed of Ice Daggers. The projectile from the enhanced version pierces all enemies in its path, increasing Ice Dagger's crowd control potential when enemies are lined up. It can also be effective at sniping enemies from range due to how often they can be shot out. The forwards motion and knockback while you cast Ice Dagger can enable you to push around groups of enemies to bunch them up, or move them into hazards.
- **Wiki additional notes:** [DOCUMENTED] Ice Dagger, Cardice Prime, and Snowflake Chakrams are the only Ice-based Water arcana that do not inflict freeze.

## 14. Rip Tide

- **Gate 1 element:** Water
- **Gate 1 category:** Basic
- **Evidence:** documented-online
- **Evidence label:** [DOCUMENTED]
- **Source:** [Wizard of Legend Wiki — Rip Tide](https://wizardoflegend.fandom.com/wiki/Rip_Tide)
- **Wiki element:** Water
- **Wiki type:** Basic
- **Wiki subtypes:** Projectile
- **Wiki stats:** Element: Water; Type: Basic; Subtypes: Projectile; Damage: 8, 7, 5; Hit Count: 5 per combo; Knockback: 5; Cooldown: 0; Cost: 12 50; Pool: 3
- **Wiki enhanced stats:** Damage: 8, 7, 5
- **Wiki behavior:** [DOCUMENTED] Unleash a quick burst of 3 water ripples in front of you with ripples dealing 8, 7 and 5 damage respectively. The ripples also destroy enemy projectiles that they impact. If enhanced, two additional ripples are sent out alongside the final ripple, which spread out in an arc as they move away. This increases the coverage area and additional damage if enemies are point blank. Unlike all other basic arcana, Rip Tide preforms a uninterruptable combo when the attack button is pressed once, and you cannot move your aim when preforming a combo.
- **Wiki enhanced behavior:** [DOCUMENTED] Final ripple is now a spreadshot! If enhanced, two additional ripples are sent out alongside the final ripple, which spread out in an arc as they move away. This increases the coverage area and additional damage if enemies are point blank.
- **Wiki strategy notes:** [DOCUMENTED] Rip Tide is unique among basic arcana since it is defensively oriented and is primarily designed for destroying incoming enemy projectiles. The fully automatic nature and rapid execution of a combo allow you to take advantage of the quick start-up inherent to most basic arcana block incoming projectiles on the fly, between casts of other arcana. When utilizing riptide in this way you need to pay very close attention to your surroundings and enemy telegraph markers (such as those of Archer) to ensure you properly time Rip Tide and successfully defend yourself from damage. The spread shot from the enhanced version drastically widens Rip Tide's coverage area allowing you to block more projectiles in a single cast and requires less precise aiming than the unenhanced version. The wider area allows you to block and entire volley of Mage and Burst Mage projectiles, however this requires even tighter timing since the spread shot comes at the end of a cast. The full execution of a combo with a single button press makes it very easy to rapidly spam, allowing you to keep enemies stun-locked with ease. This paired with the knockback of the ripples makes Rip Tide an all around good Arcana to keep large groups of enemies at bay. Try to tightly group up enemies in order to maximize damage since each ripple will pierce through all enemies.
- **Wiki additional notes:** [DOCUMENTED] You are unable to move or adjust your aim during the attack animation. This spell should be more prioritized with a defensive/armor build. A rip tide is a strong, localized, and narrow current of water which moves directly away from the shore, dragging anything caught in it out to sea.
