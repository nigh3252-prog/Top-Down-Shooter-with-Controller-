68 · STORM DRAFT — AIR · SIGNATURE

Showcase window 8:05–8:10 base, 8:10–8:16 charged signature.

SubtypesProjectile
Damage20, then 30 on a wall slam
Hit count1 (2)
Knockback0, -10
Cooldown4.5s per charge · 2 charges
Cost21 150 · Pool 4

Documented behaviour. Passively builds charges to a maximum of 2. Each cast spends one charge, releasing a draft of air in the aimed direction that deals 20 damage on impact and pushes enemies along with it. If the draft pushes an enemy against a wall, that enemy takes an additional 30 damage and is stunned.

Charged signature. Releases 4 volleys of 7 drafts each in quick succession.

Enhanced (documented, not simulated here). The burst of air destroys all enemy projectiles in its path. The showcase room has no enemy fire, so there is nothing for it to act on.

Read from the video. Drafts appear roughly one tile ahead of the caster — the point-blank blind spot the wiki warns about. Contact prints a white 20; the wall slam prints a red starburst number over a cyan shock ring. The charged release opens with a white radial spike burst on the caster, then the volleys sweep out as tall curtains of crescent wisps that pin the whole room flat against the far wall.

75 · WHIRLING WIND AGENT — AIR · STANDARD

Showcase window 9:12–9:23.

SubtypesMelee, summon
Damage7 documented · 5 on screen
Hit count~9
Knockback10
Cooldown15s · 10s duration
Cost23 200 · Pool 4

Documented behaviour. Conjures 2 agents that charge through enemies. Each charge deals damage and charges happen about once a second. The agents stay active for 10 seconds, loosely following the caster and teleporting back if the caster strays too far. Agents have a set amount of health and can be killed by enemy attacks. Their charges pierce, so a lined-up group takes several hits at once.

Enhanced (documented). Agents move and attack more quickly.

The wiki lists 7 damage per charge. The showcase capture prints 5 above every hit (and 10 when both agents connect on the same beat). This build uses the on-screen value, because matching the video was the brief.

Read from the video. Two agents spawn on the caster, each carrying a small two-part bar — red over blue — which is why they are drawn with one here. They drift near the caster between charges, then dash straight through a target and past it. Enemies thrown into a wall by that knockback trigger the same cyan slam burst the drafts cause.

WALL SLAMS

Knocking a target into a wall is a global reaction in the source, not a Storm Draft exclusive: the showcase shows the cyan burst during the agent segment too. Storm Draft carries its documented 30 bonus; other knockback scales with impact speed.

CONTROLS

  • Left half — drag anywhere to move.
  • Right half — drag anywhere to aim. Without an aim drag you face the way you run.
  • Storm Draft — tap to cast, hold until the bar fills for the charged signature.
  • Whirling Wind Agent — tap to summon both agents.
  • Desktop — WASD to move, mouse to aim, J Storm Draft (hold to charge), K agents, R reset.
