Area is not one variable. This report separates the largest single collision footprint, the unique floor swept over a deterministic cast, and the threat envelope of agents/homing formations. Repeating nine shots down one lane raises density, not automatically nine times the area.

Runtime findings

What the geometry says

  • Zones currently dominate broad, reliable coverage. Grasping Earth is the largest implemented footprint at approximately 265.90 square units, followed by Heroic Leap, Blazing Lariat, Ward of Flames, Dragon Blast, Aqua Breaker, and Searing Crown.
  • Traveling carriers are generally long but narrow. Their median practical reach is approximately 12.08 units, while their median largest instantaneous footprint is only about 1.95 square units. Long narrow cards should benefit more from pin, root, freeze, grouping, and forced alignment.
  • Movement Arcana are not inherently low-area. Heroic Leap, Blazing Lariat, Wave Front, Shearing Chain, and Tectonic Drill combine route movement with meaningful coverage, so mobility and area should be valued separately.
  • Multiplicity is not area. Perforating Jet and Bouncing Blaze mainly add repeated pressure in a reused corridor; Rock N’ Roll creates genuinely separated lanes. Bubble Barrage remains stochastic and needs runtime telemetry.

Setup and payoff cohorts

Clear setup producers

Water Prison, Gust Burst, Razor Burst, Toxic Trap, Snare Track, Frost Feint, Arcane Intervention, Mentis Imperium, Toxic Bolas, and Grasping Earth create a combat state another Arcana can consume.

High-floor engine candidates

Homing Flares, Rapid Fire Agent, Whirling Wind Agent, Earth Stomp Agent, Earthen Aegis, Ignition Rush, Dragon Blast, and Aqua Vortex should be checked first for dominance after damage, cooldown, resource, control, and defense are joined to the geometry.

These are evaluation cohorts, not final power rankings.

Design implication: These conditional cards have the clearest case for a high conditional ceiling. Their special payoff should be noticeably better than what a straightforward generalist Arcana receives when the player actually creates the required situation.

Important implementation findings

  • Selected curated runtimes override nearby generic specifications for Bladed Vine, Spark Contact, Gust Burst, Razor Burst, Thunder Line, and Circuit Line. The dispatcher-selected implementation is authoritative.
  • Terra Ring, Grasping Earth, Shock Nova, and Star Bolt receive an additional baked 2× standalone-source scale at Arcana Size 1. Audit these before balancing around their current areas and reaches.
  • Spark Contact’s live curated port animates a source-local lunge but does not advance the player. Shearing Chain’s collision route also stops around 3.4 units while the player dash reaches about 6.6.
  • Returning attacks do not share one hit policy: Cyclone Boomerang gives outbound and return legs independent ledgers, while Rock-Solid Tomahawk uses one shared target set.
