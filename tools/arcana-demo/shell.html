Source analysis · Wizard of Legend arcana showcase

Every beat below was read frame-by-frame off media/wizard-of-legend/wizard-of-legend-arcana-showcase-480p.mp4, the archived showcase in this repository, at the windows the Gate 1 video inventory assigns to each arcana. The 3D reconstruction reproduces the choreography, counts, cadence, and damage read off those frames.

Rock N' Roll — 12:05 to 12:17

Earth · Signature · catalog id UseEarthWheel. Documented behaviour: “Form rocky buzzsaws that tear forward!” Enhanced: “Forms an additional buzzsaw!”

  • Base cast (12:05–12:11). Short crouch, then rock erupts at the caster's feet and two buzzsaws tear forward in parallel lanes, slightly splayed apart.
  • Each saw is a half-buried churning rock wheel. It carves a dark scorched furrow that lingers a beat and fades, throwing dirt backwards the whole way.
  • Saws pass straight through targets rather than stopping — repeated 4-damage ticks with a white star spark per hit, and light knockback.
  • Travel runs roughly two thirds of the screen before the saws break apart.
  • Charged cast (12:11–12:17). Holding the cast plants the caster and rings them with long, flickering white spikes lying flat on the floor, growing as the charge builds.
  • On release the ground erupts into a tall stacked fan of five saws that roll forward together, ticking 5 damage each and shredding a whole column of dummies at once.

Earth Stomp Agent — 12:41 to 12:53

Earth · Standard · Melee, summon. Documented: 15 damage, ~9 hits, knockback 20/10, 15s cooldown, pool 4. Enhanced adds an immediate second 10-damage stomp.

  • Summon. Rock bursts beside the caster and leaves a hovering agent — a glowing olive core inside a ring of tan rock shards, with its own small health bar.
  • It idles with a bob and loosely follows the caster, teleporting back if left too far behind.
  • Stomp cadence ≈ 1.1s. The agent leaps at the nearest enemy; airborne it flares its shards into a wide spinning star while its shadow stays and shrinks on the floor.
  • Impact. Radial cracks split the stone in a wide ring, dust and rock chunks fly out, everything in radius takes 15 and is shoved hard away. The crack decal stays on the floor and fades slowly.
  • The agent lives 10 seconds, then crumbles.

Controls

  • Drag anywhere on the left to move. Facing follows movement.
  • Tap an arcana to cast. Hold Rock N' Roll to charge, release to fire.
  • ▶ replays the source showcase order: three base casts, then two charged casts, then the agent summon.
  • Gamepad: left stick moves, □/X is Rock N' Roll (hold to charge), △/Y is Earth Stomp Agent.
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows, J and K.
